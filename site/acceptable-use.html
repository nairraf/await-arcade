Acceptable Use

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This is a beta-2 policy draft for review before launch. It is not legal advice. If anything is unclear, contact support@awaitarcade.com.

Play fair

  • Do not cheat, automate play, modify the client, forge replays, or exploit bugs to gain a score.
  • Do not use multiple accounts to evade limits or manipulate boards.
  • Do not scrape, overload, or attempt to bypass rate limits on the API.

Be decent

  • No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, doxxing, or impersonation.
  • No offensive, misleading, or infringing arcade display names.
  • No spam, malware, phishing, or illegal activity.

Security testing

Follow the Security Policy. Good-faith research under that policy is welcome; do not test third-party services, access other people’s data, or disrupt the service. Report issues to security@awaitarcade.com.

Enforcement

We may rename a player, remove a board entry, reset XP, suspend an account, or delete an account. Serious or repeated violations may be reported to the relevant platform or authority.

To report abuse, email abuse@awaitarcade.com with the player name, board, and what happened. Appeals can go to support@awaitarcade.com.
