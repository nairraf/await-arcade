Built for the wait

Verified, shared, and a little competitive

Replay-verified

Every ranked game sends your seed and keystrokes, not a raw number. The server replays your exact run so high scores actually mean something.

Shared boards

Top 50 per cartridge and mode, with your rank and global XP standing.

Daily challenge

One featured cartridge a day, three attempts, best of three on a fixed seed.

XP, medals, ghosts

Earn XP from your best run each day, collect medals as you level up, or click Practice beside any top score to replay that exact board unranked.

Two hosts, one arcade

Play where the agent works

Keyboard input stays scoped to the game pane so it never hijacks your chat box — and your player profile follows you between editors.

OpenCode Desktop

Lives in a native OpenCode tab with a header pill to jump in and out while a session runs. Uses OpenCode’s experimental plugin GUI API.

Antigravity

Runs in an auxiliary sidecar pane right beside your editor. Same GitHub login, same XP, same boards. Currently macOS-first.

Zero friction

Quick to start, zero tracking

Quick in, easy out

Open a game right inside your editor and drop back to your diff the instant your agent finishes. Turn-based games wait on your next move; action runs stay lightweight. One script installs it, and one flag cleanly removes everything if you ever want it gone.

No tracking, no account required

No ads, no analytics scripts, no tracking cookies, and nothing phones home while you play offline. Guest scores stay on your machine — GitHub sign-in is strictly optional if you want to compete on the shared boards. Open source under the MIT license by Farrworks.
