Accessibility

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This is a beta-2 policy draft for review before launch. It is not legal advice. If anything is unclear, contact support@awaitarcade.com.

Our approach

The website targets WCAG 2.2 AA and works with keyboard navigation, browser zoom, forced-colors mode, and reduced motion. It uses semantic HTML and visible focus styles. The games expose accessible labels for cells and controls.

Known limitations

  • Neon Cycles and Brickstorm use canvas; some assistive technology may not expose their internal state.
  • Host webviews can override fonts, contrast, and zoom behavior.
  • The beta has not yet had a full independent accessibility audit.

Feedback

Tell us what blocks you at support@awaitarcade.com. Include the host, operating system, and the assistive technology you use.
