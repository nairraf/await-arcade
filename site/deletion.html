Data Deletion

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This is a beta-2 policy draft for review before launch. It is not legal advice. If anything is unclear, contact hello@awaitarcade.com.

Delete from the app

  1. Open the account panel.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.
  3. The host clears its local session and returns to guest mode.

This deletes your player record, GitHub provider mapping, sessions, access tokens, pending runs, challenge attempts, ghosts, XP records, and every board entry. Your display name disappears from the boards.

What is not deleted

  • Your GitHub account is not deleted or changed.
  • Local bests, settings, and queued runs can remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall.
  • Service logs may retain limited request metadata for up to 30 days for security and operations, then expire. We do not configure separate backup restores.

Email request

If you cannot use the in-app control, email privacy@awaitarcade.com from the address associated with your request. We may ask you to verify control of the GitHub account before deleting data. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Clear local host data

Sign out in the account panel, then remove the host’s auth file, queue, and local bests if you want a clean device. The packaged installer will include an uninstall path in beta-2.
