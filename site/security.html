Security Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This is a beta-2 policy draft for review before launch. It is not legal advice. If anything is unclear, contact support@awaitarcade.com.

Supported versions

Security fixes target the current beta build (the latest development head and the beta-1 tag) of the API, website, and official host builds. Older builds are not supported.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@awaitarcade.com with:

  • a description and impact;
  • steps to reproduce, including URLs and accounts;
  • any proof-of-concept or logs, with secrets redacted;
  • your preferred contact and whether you want credit.

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Do not access, modify, or delete other people’s data, and do not disrupt the service.

What to expect

  • We aim to acknowledge reports within 2 business days.
  • We aim to triage within 7 days and fix confirmed issues as soon as practical.
  • We will coordinate disclosure after a fix; please give us reasonable time.

Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that follows this policy, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and is reported privately. This is not a bug bounty and does not authorize testing third-party services.

Scope

In scope: awaitarcade.com, the Await Arcade API, the official OpenCode and Antigravity clients, and the public source repository. Out of scope: GitHub, Google Cloud, Zoho, denial-of-service testing, spam, social engineering, and physical attacks.

Machine-readable contact

See /.well-known/security.txt.
